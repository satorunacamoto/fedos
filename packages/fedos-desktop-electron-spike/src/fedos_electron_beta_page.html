Capabilities

    Beta limitations

      Conversations

        Invite a contact

        Sending an invitation creates a new Direct Conversation and delivers it over your configured Relay. There is no code for your contact to paste — the conversation appears in their own list automatically once they are unlocked and connected.

        Conversation

          Search

          Search matches locally-held plaintext using a keyword index derived from your own messages. This is not an encrypted blind index — see Safety Center for details.

            Archive

            Export a Direct Conversation

            Exporting does not remove or invalidate your live, active encrypted session — restoring history later never resumes that live session automatically.

            Restore an Archive

            Restore applies your current Retention/suppression state — some restored items may be reported as redacted or tombstoned rather than fully recovered. A Device that never received a related deletion/suppression fact remains outside this guarantee.

            Devices

              Revoking a Device removes its trust for future messages — it does not delete copies that Device already has, and does not affect Relay-side or foreign Archive copies.

              Safety Center

              Every section below is reported independently. There is no overall security score — a partial or unavailable section does not mean the rest of the application is unsafe, and a fully "supported" section is not an external audit result.

                Identity Replacement

                A pending item means a contact's account signed a NEW identity claiming continuity with an existing conversation. Never auto-accepted — review it yourself before trusting it.

                  Signed Deletion requests

                  A signed request to delete something locally. Approving it never guarantees removal from a Relay, another Device, or a foreign Archive copy — this policy always requires your own review.
