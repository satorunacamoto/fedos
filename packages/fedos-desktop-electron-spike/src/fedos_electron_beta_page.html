                      Archive

                      Export a Direct Conversation

                      Exporting does not remove or invalidate your live, active encrypted session — restoring history later never resumes that live session automatically.

                      Restore an Archive

                      Restore applies your current Retention/suppression state — some restored items may be reported as redacted or tombstoned rather than fully recovered. A Device that never received a related deletion/suppression fact remains outside this guarantee.

                      Devices

                        Revoking a Device removes its trust for future messages — it does not delete copies that Device already has, and does not affect Relay-side or foreign Archive copies.

                        Pair a New Device

                        Pairing has three steps across two Devices — the NEW Device (this one has its own separate local profile) and your EXISTING, already-trusted Device that holds your account. Pairing trusts this Device under that account for Sync; it does not merge or replace either Device's own local profile. Do this step first on the NEW Device you want to add.

                        Step 1 — On the NEW Device: create a pairing request

                        Send this request to your existing, trusted Device by any channel you already trust (it proves only this new Device's own key — it carries no secret). It expires; if it expires before you finish, start over.

                        Keep this application open until you complete Step 3 below — the request is lost if you quit before finishing.

                        Step 2 — On the EXISTING, trusted Device: inspect and approve

                        Approving trusts this Device for future Sync and messaging with this account, and cannot be undone from here (only Revoke, afterward, can remove trust again).

                        Send this approval back to the new Device. Approving here also trusts the new Device locally on THIS Device right away.

                        Step 3 — Back on the NEW Device: complete pairing

                        Pairing trusts this Device for future Sync and messaging with this account. It does not itself prove the other Device has erased anything, and offline does not mean revoked.

                        Safety Center

                        Every section below is reported independently. There is no overall security score — a partial or unavailable section does not mean the rest of the application is unsafe, and a fully "supported" section is not an external audit result.

                          Identity Replacement

                          A pending item means a contact's account signed a NEW identity claiming continuity with an existing conversation. Never auto-accepted — review it yourself before trusting it.

                            Signed Deletion requests

                            A signed request to delete something locally. Approving it never guarantees removal from a Relay, another Device, or a foreign Archive copy — this policy always requires your own review.

                              Settings

                              This is a Closed Beta build. Settings changes are saved locally to this profile only — they are not backed up, synced, or recoverable if this profile is lost.

                              Relays

                              A Relay operator (or a network observer) can see who you are messaging and when, even though it cannot read message contents. Adding a Relay you do not trust exposes this metadata to it.

                              Preferences

                              This saves your preference, but the interface text itself is English-only in this Beta — no translated interface exists yet.

                              Relay changes require a restart of this application to take effect. Nothing is applied until you press Save.

                              Commerce Nostr Publication (experimental)

                              Optional. Publishing Commerce listings to Nostr makes them visible to relay operators and anyone else observing the relay. Local Only (the default) never reaches the network.

                              This choice is saved to this device and stays in effect across restarts, until you change it again.
